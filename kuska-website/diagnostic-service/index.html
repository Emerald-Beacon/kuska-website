Autism Evaluations

Autism diagnosis support in Utah, with a clear and parent-friendly process.

If you’re worried about communication, social development, or behavior, a professional autism evaluation can help your family understand what’s going on and what support makes sense next.

Get answers sooner

Families come to Kuska when they want more clarity around developmental differences and need a path forward that feels informed rather than confusing.

Access support

A diagnosis can open the door to ABA therapy, school accommodations, insurance-covered care, and more coordinated planning.

Build a strong foundation

Earlier understanding often means earlier intervention, which can support communication, regulation, and independence.

What to Expect

A four-step process that keeps families informed.

1

Clinical interview

We learn about concerns, developmental history, and day-to-day patterns.

2

Observation and testing

Professional observation helps us understand strengths, support needs, and behavior in context.

3

Clear report

Families receive a diagnosis summary and practical recommendations they can actually use.

4

Connection to next steps

If ABA therapy or other services are appropriate, we help map out what happens after the evaluation.

Good to Know

Answers to the practical questions parents ask about autism testing.

No waitlist for inquiries

Evaluation inquiries have no waitlist at Kuska. Reach out and we’ll help you understand scheduling, availability, and what happens next.

Insurance coverage in Utah

Utah Medicaid covers autism services for eligible members regardless of age, and Utah’s autism insurance law requires many plans to cover ASD diagnosis. Kuska is in-network with Utah Medicaid, Select Health, Optum, UnitedHealthcare, Evernorth, and Blue Cross Blue Shield.

Testing insurers look for

Many Utah insurers, including Medicaid, require a diagnosis that includes an ADOS assessment before approving ABA therapy. We’ll walk you through what your plan requires when you schedule.

Co-Occurring Conditions

Autism & co-occurring conditions.

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) can occur alongside a variety of developmental, behavioral, neurological, and medical conditions. At Kuska Autism Services, we recognize that every individual is unique and that understanding the whole person is an important part of providing individualized care. Select a condition below to read more.

ADHD

Difficulty with attention, impulsivity, hyperactivity, and executive functioning.

Anxiety

Excessive worry, fear, avoidance, or difficulty coping with uncertainty and change.

Depression

Changes in mood, interest, energy, sleep, appetite, or social engagement.

Intellectual Disability

Differences in intellectual functioning and adaptive skills.

Epilepsy & Seizure Disorders

Changes in awareness, staring spells, unusual movements, or seizures.

Sleep Difficulties

Difficulty falling asleep, staying asleep, or maintaining a consistent sleep schedule.

Feeding & Eating Difficulties

Food selectivity, sensory sensitivities, or difficulty with textures and new foods.

Speech & Language Differences

Difficulty expressing needs, understanding language, or participating in conversation.

Motor & Coordination Difficulties

Challenges with balance, coordination, handwriting, and gross and fine motor skills.

Gastrointestinal Concerns

Constipation, diarrhea, reflux, abdominal discomfort, or other digestive concerns.

Sensory Processing Differences

Increased or decreased responses to sounds, touch, lights, smells, tastes, or movement.

At Kuska, our clinical team considers each individual’s strengths, needs, communication, environment, and goals when developing services. When additional concerns are identified, we encourage families to work with the appropriate medical, developmental, or behavioral professionals to ensure their child receives comprehensive support.

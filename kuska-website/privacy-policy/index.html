Privacy Policy

How Kuska Autism Services handles personal information, website data, and protected health information.

How we handle the information you share with us online, and how we protect health information as a HIPAA-covered provider.

Effective date: July 7, 2026

Who we are

Kuska Autism Services ("Kuska," "we," "us") provides autism evaluations and ABA therapy at clinics in Bountiful and Draper, Utah. This policy explains what information is collected when you use this website (kuska.co), how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to the website only — the privacy of your health information as a patient or client is governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices, which we provide during intake.

The short version

  • This website does not store patient records or protected health information.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics tools, and the site itself does not set tracking cookies.
  • Information you submit through our contact and intake forms goes to our team through a secure form provider — not into this website.
  • We never sell or rent your information to anyone.

Information you share with us

The contact and "Get Started" forms on this site are provided by Zoho Forms, a third-party service embedded in the page. When you submit a form, your responses — such as your name, contact details, and basic information about your child and scheduling needs — are transmitted directly to Zoho and delivered to our administrative team. This website does not save or store your submission.

Please share only the details we need to follow up with you. A form or email is a fine way to start a conversation, but it is not a secure channel for detailed medical, insurance, or diagnostic information — we will gather anything sensitive by phone or in person through HIPAA-appropriate channels. If you email us at admin@kuska.co or call (801) 980-7970, we use what you share only to respond to you.

Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs — such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — which we may review for security and to keep the site working. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers receive your IP address when the page loads. Beyond that, this site does not run analytics, advertising pixels, or fingerprinting of any kind.

Cookies

This website does not set tracking cookies. Embedded third-party content — such as the Zoho form on our contact and Get Started pages — may set cookies that are necessary for the form to function. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings.

How we use your information

We use the information you send us to respond to your questions, schedule consultations and evaluations, and determine whether our services are a fit for your family. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not add you to marketing lists you didn't ask for.

HIPAA and protected health information

Kuska Autism Services is a HIPAA-covered health care provider. Protected health information (PHI) that we create or receive in the course of providing care is safeguarded as required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act and applicable Utah law, and its use is described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

This website is informational. It does not host patient portals, treatment records, or clinical documentation, and no PHI is stored on it. Website inquiries are handled by our administrative team, and any clinical information you later share with us is managed within our HIPAA-compliant systems, not on this site.

When we share information

We share information only with the service providers that make this website work — our form provider (Zoho) and our website host — and only to the extent needed to operate the site and respond to you. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. We never sell personal information.

Children's privacy

Our services support children, but this website is intended for parents, guardians, and other adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information through this site, contact us and we will delete it.

Retention and security

Inquiry information is kept only as long as needed to respond to you and, if you become a client, to support your care — at which point it is managed under our clinical record-keeping policies. We limit access to inquiry information to team members who need it to help you.

Your choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold from your website inquiries, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. You can also ask us to stop contacting you. Requests for access to clinical records are handled under HIPAA through the process described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Links to other sites

This site links to third-party services such as Google Maps (for directions to our clinics), Facebook, and Instagram. Those services have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the website handles information, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
